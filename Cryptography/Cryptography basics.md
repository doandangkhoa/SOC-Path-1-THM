# Types of Encryption

## Symmetric Encryption

also known as `private key cryptography`

DES, 3DES, AES.

## Asymmetric Encryption

it is also called **public key cryptography**.

Examples are RSA, Diffie-Hellman, and Elliptic Curve cryptography (ECC
